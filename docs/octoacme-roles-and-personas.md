# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to clarify priorities and refine acceptance criteria.
- Work with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Collaborate with UX/UI Designers on implementation feasibility and user flows.
- Coordinate with the Technical Lead/Architect on design decisions and technical standards.
- Support QA/Testing and Release/Operations or SRE during validation and deployment.

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers research user needs and shape accessible, understandable, and consistent workflows and interfaces.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and usability testing
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interface specifications
- Define interaction patterns and accessibility expectations
- Validate designs against implementation and acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer value
- Ensure a consistent and accessible experience
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated workflows

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Collaboration sessions with Developers and Technical Leads
- Acceptance validation with QA/Testing

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align user needs and success metrics with the Product Manager.
- Provide implementation-ready designs and clarify trade-offs with Developers.
- Coordinate usability and accessibility coverage with QA/Testing.
- Share stakeholder insights through the Project Manager and other stakeholder communications.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Set outcomes and priorities with Stakeholders and the Project Manager.
- Work with UX/UI Designers and Business Analysts to translate needs into usable requirements.
- Review technical trade-offs with the Technical Lead/Architect and Developers.
- Agree on acceptance evidence with QA/Testing and Release/Operations or SRE.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align scope and milestones with the Product Manager and Stakeholders.
- Facilitate planning and escalate cross-team dependencies with Developers and Technical Leads.
- Ensure design, requirements, testing, and release work is included in plans and tracked to completion.
- Coordinate operational readiness and incident communication with Release/Operations or SRE.

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, ensure coherent design, and help teams manage technical quality and risk.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and review technical designs and standards
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and constraints
- Guide implementation decisions and design reviews
- Support estimates, integration planning, and non-functional requirements

### Goals
- Build secure, reliable, and maintainable systems
- Resolve technical dependencies early
- Maintain architectural consistency while enabling iterative delivery

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Planning and technical risk discussions
- Consultation with Developers and delivery leads

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to understand trade-offs and constraints.
- Advise Developers and review implementation decisions.
- Coordinate technical risks and dependencies with the Project Manager.
- Partner with UX/UI Designers on feasible interaction and performance decisions.
- Align quality and operability expectations with QA/Testing and Release/Operations or SRE.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify stakeholder needs and translate them into consistent requirements and acceptance criteria that delivery teams can implement and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business and stakeholder requirements
- Document workflows, rules, assumptions, and dependencies
- Refine requirements and acceptance criteria
- Resolve ambiguity and trace decisions to project outcomes

### Goals
- Improve requirement completeness and clarity
- Reduce conflicting interpretations and late rework
- Connect delivery work to measurable business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria reviews
- Clarification sessions with delivery and QA teams

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Stakeholders and the Product Manager to validate needs and priorities.
- Collaborate with UX/UI Designers to document end-to-end workflows.
- Provide clear requirements and edge cases to Developers and QA/Testing.
- Surface requirement dependencies and decision needs to the Project Manager.

---

## QA/Testing

### Role Summary
QA/Testing roles validate that delivered work meets functional, usability, security, reliability, and acceptance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test approaches and acceptance validation
- Create and maintain automated and manual test coverage
- Report defects and verify fixes
- Identify quality, usability, and release risks

### Goals
- Prevent defects and quality regressions
- Provide evidence that acceptance criteria are met
- Improve testability and release confidence

### Typical Communication
- Refinement and test-plan reviews
- Daily delivery updates and defect triage
- Release readiness reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases with the Product Manager and Business Analyst.
- Coordinate testability and defect resolution with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect.
- Validate UX/accessibility expectations with UX/UI Designers.
- Provide quality evidence and rollout concerns to the Project Manager and Release/Operations or SRE.

---

## Release/Operations or SRE

### Role Summary
Release/Operations and Site Reliability Engineering roles ensure services are deployable, observable, resilient, and operable in production.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and automate deployments and rollback procedures
- Define monitoring, alerting, and service-level expectations
- Participate in capacity and operational readiness reviews
- Coordinate incident response and post-release verification

### Goals
- Reduce deployment and operational risk
- Improve availability, observability, and recovery
- Create a repeatable and auditable release process

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and operational readiness reviews
- Deployment and incident handoffs
- Monitoring and capacity reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate release scope, timing, and rollout plans with the Project Manager.
- Align operational requirements and runbooks with Developers and the Technical Lead/Architect.
- Include reliability and environment checks in QA/Testing plans.
- Communicate production readiness and incidents to the Product Manager and Stakeholders.

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide domain knowledge, approvals, priorities, or operational context needed to guide project decisions and adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate needs, constraints, and policy or business requirements
- Review proposals and provide timely decisions
- Validate outcomes and support adoption
- Escalate unresolved concerns through agreed communication channels

### Goals
- Ensure delivered work supports business and operational needs
- Provide clear decisions and reduce delivery ambiguity
- Support timely adoption and benefit realization

### Typical Communication
- Kickoffs, milestone reviews, and status updates
- Requirements and design validation sessions
- Release and change announcements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Set priorities and validate outcomes with the Product Manager.
- Supply context and decisions to the Business Analyst and UX/UI Designer.
- Receive plans, risks, and escalation updates from the Project Manager.
- Review progress and readiness with QA/Testing and Release/Operations or SRE when needed.

---

## Applying Roles to a Project

A project should assign named accountability for the responsibilities above. Roles may be held by separate people or combined when a team is small; combining roles does not remove the responsibility, it only means one person may serve in more than one role. When roles are combined or omitted, document the accountable owner in the project plan and retain independent review for high-risk decisions when practical.
